## 📝 Product Overview
**AutoBell** is a next-generation, cloud-based bell management system designed specifically for schools, colleges, and madrasas. Unlike old-fashioned mechanical timers, AutoBell connects to your existing sound system and allows you to manage schedules, upload custom audio, and trigger emergency alerts directly from a **Web Dashboard** or **Mobile App**.

It works offline, keeps perfect time via the internet, and offers flexible profiles for exams, half-days, or Ramadan schedules.

---

## 🏆 Key Benefits (Why Choose AutoBell?)

*   **📱 Total Control Anywhere:** Manage your bells from a laptop or your smartphone. No need to run to the control room.
*   **⚡ Zero Downtime:** The device stores schedules locally. Even if the internet goes down, your bells still ring on time.
*   **📅 Flexible Scheduling:** Easily switch between "Regular Day," "Exam Mode," or "Rainy Day" profiles with one tap.
*   **🔊 Custom Audio:** Upload your own MP3s. Play school anthems, specific announcements, or Adhan for prayer times.
*   **🚨 Safety First:** Instantly trigger emergency alarms or lockdowns from the mobile app in case of fire or security threats.
*   **💾 Full Data Backup & Restore:** Keep your schedules, logs, and MP3 files completely safe with our comprehensive system backup tool.
*   **🤖 Hardware Watchdog:** Built-in auto-recovery ensures 100% uptime without manual restarts even if the device encounters a glitch.
*   **🛠️ Local Support & Backup Device:** We offer an optional yearly agreement that includes a backup device service, ensuring your school never goes silent.

## 📋 Technical Specs (For IT Admins)
*   **Controller:** ESP32-based (High performance, WiFi enabled).
*   **Audio Output:** High-quality DAC/I2S output to Amplifier.
*   **Time Sync:** NTP (Network Time Protocol) for second-level precision.
*   **Power:** Standard 5V/12V input (depending on model).
*   **Connectivity:** 2.4GHz WiFi.
*   **Platform:** React Web Dashboard + React Native Mobile App (Android).
